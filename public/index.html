Backup & restore

Your trips are stored only on this phone. Each night after 10pm the app reminds you to save a backup file. Save it to the same place each time (e.g. iCloud Drive or Google Drive) and choose Replace — it's always called mileage-backup.zip (trips, expenses and receipt photos), so you keep one up-to-date copy.

How automatic reports work

A report is made after each week (Mon–Sun), month, quarter (Jan–Mar, Apr–Jun, Jul–Sep, Oct–Dec) and financial year (Jul–Jun) that has trips — at midnight if the app is open, otherwise the next time you open it. Time zone: .
